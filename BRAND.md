# Hullwater brand decisions

The founder's brief is the only source of facts. If she didn't say it, the page doesn't claim it.

## Voice

**Aim for:** calm, clean, honest, warm

- Short, plain sentences. Say what it is: "barley and water."
- Quiet confidence. No exclamation points, no hype, no urgency.
- Warm the way the grandmother's story is warm: specific and real, not sentimental.

**Avoid:** revolution, hydration, elevate, journey, transform, boost, unlock, wellness, superfood, detox, game-changer, "not just ___", "loved by thousands", "trusted by", any health or performance claim, emoji, exclamation points

## What the page says

**What it is:** cold-brewed barley tea in a can. Four ingredients, nothing hiding. It started with the founder's grandmother, who made barley tea every summer from barley and water.

**Who it's for:** people who get back from a run and want something clean to drink, and anyone tired of a drinks aisle that's either sugar or aggressively healthy.

**What the visitor should do:** buy it online.

**Theme:** something to reach for after a run, the way her grandmother's tea was something to reach for every summer. Calm, not a promise.

**Not on the page** (not in the brief): health benefits (gut, skin, digestion, energy, focus), glass packaging, wellness or supplement positioning, pack sizes, prices, stats, testimonials, partner logos.

**Need from the founder before launch:**
- The other two ingredients (she said four but only named barley and water)
- Price and how it's sold
- Her name, for the footer
